Our Story

INFRATECH

INFRATECH was officially registered in 2021, founded by a team of seasoned infrastructure and IT specialists with over 15 years of industry experience.Formerly holding senior roles at IPMC Ghana,including Head of Infrastructure and Lead IT Technician,our team has managed and delivered mission-critical projects across enterprise networks, structured cabling, surveillance systems, and access control environments.

With deep technical expertise and a track record of high-performance execution, INFRATECH was established to provide smart, secure, and scalable infrastructure solutions that meet modern business demands.
